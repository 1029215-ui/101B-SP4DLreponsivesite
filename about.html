About Us

We at 101 Bagels and Subs are a family-owned restaurant that has been serving Oceanside commuters, families, and neighbors for years. We focus on simple, well-made food — warm bagels, fresh fillings, and friendly service.

Our goal is to feel like your neighborhood kitchen. Our hometown restaurant serves a variety of bagels and sandwiches made with fresh ingredients, including our classic and unique bagel sandwiches and options like chicken salad, tuna salad, and roast beef.

101 Bagels and Subs is located by the Oceanside Pier and offers both indoor and patio seating, plus options for to-go orders. We have a unique selection of flavored bagels and offer gluten-free options.
